# Vendor-account takeover: fraud playbook (invented for the demo)

## How it usually goes
Failed logins and a password reset on the supplier portal from a device and country never seen before; then the
contact email and phone are changed; days later an email asks to pay all open invoices to a new IBAN before the next
payment run. Each step reaches a different team, and each looks routine on its own.

## Red flags that only add up across channels
A callback to a phone number that was put on file a few days earlier. A bank-change email from a free-mail address
that the portal now lists as the vendor's contact. A new IBAN in another country, often a money-mule account.

## What a careful attacker does
Uses the vendor's real mailbox, writes without urgency, and asks for an account in the vendor's own country under the
vendor's own name. Little or nothing looks wrong before the first payment; Verification of Payee at the bank helps.
